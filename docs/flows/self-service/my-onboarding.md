# My onboarding tasks and documents

Work through your new-hire checklist and submit the identification documents HR has asked you for, both from the Settings screen.

## Who can use this

Every signed-in Fernly user. The **My onboarding** and **My identification documents** sections only ever show your own tasks and your own documents.

## Where to find it

Sidebar → General → **Settings**, then scroll to the **My onboarding** and **My identification documents** sections (they sit below your employee profile).

## When to use it

- In your first weeks at the company, to tick off onboarding tasks as you complete them.
- Whenever HR requests an identification document from you (for example an ID or a licence).
- To check which requested documents are still outstanding.

## Before you start

- You cannot create checklists or request documents yourself — HR creates the checklist when you join and asks for documents as needed. Your part is to complete and submit.
- Onboarding tasks may be assigned to you personally, to everyone holding a role (shown as "the … role"), or left unassigned for HR to pick up.
- A document marked with a **req** badge is a mandatory document type — these are the documents HR requires to complete your joining file, and they are counted in the section's summary until they are verified.

## Steps

**Tick an onboarding task**
1. Under **My onboarding**, find the checklist and the task you finished.
2. Click the empty checkbox next to the task title.
3. To undo a tick, click the checkbox again.

**Submit an identification document**
1. Under **My identification documents**, find a document whose status is Requested or Rejected.
2. Type a reference for your file in the box with the placeholder "file reference" (for example, where you uploaded it or its file name).
3. Click **Submit**.

## What you'll see

**My onboarding** shows each checklist by title with the new hire's name, and lists only tasks that are still open — finished tasks drop off the list. Each task row shows:

- the task title, with a **req** badge when it is a required task;
- who it belongs to ("@Name", "the … role" or "unassigned");
- its due date, for example "due 14 Nov" or "due day 5" (overdue open dates are highlighted);
- "done by …" with the name of the person who ticked it, once completed.

When everything is ticked you see "All your onboarding tasks are done."; if nothing involves you: "No onboarding checklists involve you."

**My identification documents** opens with a summary line such as "3 of 5 verified · 2 outstanding" (or "no identification documents on file"). Each row shows the document name, the **req** badge for mandatory types, the file reference you submitted (or "no file reference"), who verified it, any remarks from HR, and a status: **Requested**, **Received**, **Verified** or **Rejected**. The submit box appears only on Requested and Rejected rows.

## Common problems

**A task you finished is no longer shown.**
That is expected — the section lists open tasks only. HR still sees the full checklist with every tick.

**You cannot tick a task.**
> Only the task's assignee, a holder of its role, the new hire, or HR can tick this task.

If you believe you should be able to tick it, ask HR to check who the task is assigned to.

**You submitted a document but nothing seems to happen.**
The row moves to **Received** and now waits for HR. Submitting again is refused:
> Document is already RECEIVED.

(The message names the current status — Received, Verified and so on.)

**A document shows Rejected.**
HR's note on the row explains what was wrong. The submit box reappears — submit a corrected file reference and the document goes back to **Received** for a fresh check.

**You want to change the document itself.**
You only submit the reference here. If the file itself needs replacing, submit the new reference after HR re-requests the document, or ask HR to reset the row.

## What happens behind the scenes

- Ticking a task records who ticked it and when, and the checklist is marked complete automatically once its last open task is ticked (it reopens if a tick is undone).
- Submitting a document moves it from Requested to Received and records your submission. Verification is HR's decision — there is no way to verify your own document.
- Required tasks and mandatory document types are flagged so HR can see at a glance what still stands between you and a complete joining file.
