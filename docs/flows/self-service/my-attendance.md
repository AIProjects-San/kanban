# My attendance

See which days HR marked for you in any month — present, WFH, half day, absent or on leave — with in/out times and worked hours. The view is read-only.

## Who can use this

Every signed-in Fernly user. You see only your own attendance — never a colleague's.

## Where to find it

Sidebar → People → **My Attendance**.

## When to use it

- To check that HR's records match the days you actually worked.
- To see your in and out times, late/early flags and hours worked per day.
- To review a past month (for example before a payroll run closes).
- To reach your timesheets — the **My timesheets** link on this screen opens your timesheet page.

## Before you start

- You cannot mark or edit attendance yourself — there is no self-marking in Fernly. HR records every day, including WFH and on-leave days.
- If something is wrong, contact HR for a correction. The screen itself says so at the bottom: "Attendance is recorded by HR — including WFH and on-leave days. Contact HR for corrections; there is no self-marking."
- Days with approved leave show automatically as **On leave** — they come from your approved leave requests, not from a separate HR mark.

## Steps

**View a month**
1. Open the view — it starts on the current month.
2. Use the month picker (or the arrows either side of it) to choose another month.
3. Click **Today** to jump back to the current month.

**Check a specific day**
1. Find the day in the calendar grid (weeks run Monday to Sunday; weekends are shaded).
2. Read the status chip on the day — the same details are repeated in the "Marked days" list below, together with times and notes.

**Open your timesheets**
1. Click **My timesheets** at the top right of the screen. This opens your timesheet page, where you draft and submit your actual hours (see [My timesheets](my-timesheets.md)).

## What you'll see

- **The header** — how many days are marked in the chosen month ("12 marked days in November 2026", or "No marked days in … yet"), your name, the month controls and the **My timesheets** link.
- **Summary chips** — a count per status: **Present**, **WFH**, **Half day**, **Absent**, **On leave**.
- **The month calendar** — one cell per day; marked days carry their status chip, unmarked days stay empty, weekends are shaded.
- **Marked days list** — one row per marked day: the date ("Wed 25 Dec"), the status, and where available:
  - "from approved leave" on days derived from approved leave;
  - your in and out times ("09:00 → 17:30");
  - **Late** flags ("Late 25m") and **Early** flags ("Early 10m");
  - hours worked ("7h 30m worked");
  - any note HR added.
- If nothing is marked in the month: "Nothing marked for this month yet."

## Common problems

**A day is missing.**
Nobody has marked it yet. HR marks attendance; ask them to complete the month. (Whether an unmarked day costs pay depends on your workspace's payroll policy — HR can tell you.)

**A day is wrong.**
Contact HR — the screen is read-only on purpose, and every correction is made and recorded on their side. Quote the exact date and what you expected.

**You were on leave but the day shows something else.**
Approved leave normally shows as **On leave** with "from approved leave". If your leave was requested late or cancelled, the day may still show its old mark — ask HR to align it.

**Your in/out times look shifted.**
Times are shown exactly as recorded. If you forgot to sign in or out, HR can add or correct the times.

**The hours worked look off.**
Hours are computed from your in and out times (breaks may apply, per HR's setup). Query a specific day with HR.

## What happens behind the scenes

- HR records attendance for everyone; approved leave feeds "On leave" days in automatically, and cancelling approved leave removes them again.
- Late, early and worked-hours badges are computed from the recorded in and out times — they are facts, not edits.
- Payroll uses these marks when a month is finalized: absent and half-day marks (and unpaid leave) can become loss-of-pay on your payslip, which is one more reason to check this screen each month.
