# My payslips

See every payslip that has been finalized for you, open any of them read-only, and print or save one as a PDF.

## Who can use this

Every signed-in Fernly user. The view shows only your own payslips — there is no way to see anyone else's.

## Where to find it

Sidebar → People → **My Payslips**.

## When to use it

- To check what you were paid in a finalized month, with the full earnings and deductions breakdown.
- To confirm your year-to-date (YTD) net pay.
- To print or save a payslip for a loan application, visa or your own records.
- To see whether a month's net was held by HR and when it was released.

## Before you start

- Payslips appear here only once a month has been **finalized (locked)** by HR or an admin. A month that is still open is a draft HR may still edit — draft months are never shown.
- Everything here is read-only. If something looks wrong, contact HR; corrections are made on the payroll side.
- Fernly has no separate self-service screen for company loans. If you have a loan, its repayments simply appear as deduction lines on your payslips.

## Steps

**Open a payslip**
1. In the summary card at the top, click **View payslip** to open your latest pay — or click any row in the payslip history below.
2. Read the slip. Earnings are listed first, then deductions, then the totals.
3. Optionally pick a paper format: **Classic**, **Compact** or **Detailed**.
4. Click **Print / save PDF** to print the slip or save it as a PDF.
5. Close the window with the × (or press Esc).

## What you'll see

- **The summary card** — your **Latest pay** (net) with its month and slip number, plus **Gross**, **Deductions**, and **Net YTD** for the year with the note "(paid runs only)". A smaller line shows "this month: …" — the net paid so far in the current calendar month.
- **Payslip history** — one row per finalized payslip: slip number, month, gross and deductions, and (if any) loss-of-pay days with their source ("LOP 2d (auto)" or "manual"), the YTD net at that point, and the net amount. The list is labelled "finalized months only".
- **HELD and RELEASED badges** — a month whose net is held by HR shows **HELD** ("This month's net is held by HR — it pays when the hold is released"); once paid out it shows **RELEASED** ("This held net was released — it paid as an adjustment line in a later month").
- **Inside a payslip** — the "Earnings (monthly)" and "Deductions (monthly)" lines (loan repayments show up here as deduction lines), an optional "For information (not counted in pay)" block, the loss-of-pay note, a "Statutory (auto — NZ tax engine, frozen at payment)" block with PAYE, KiwiSaver and related lines (employer KiwiSaver is marked "not deducted from pay"), and the totals: Gross, Deductions, Net pay, YTD gross, YTD net and Month-to-date net. A note field may carry a remark shown on the printed slip.
- The footer states: "The run is locked — this slip is the paid, immutable record."

If nothing has been finalized yet: "No payslips yet. Your payslips appear here once your HR or admin finalizes (locks) the monthly payroll run. Draft months are never shown."

## Common problems

**This month's payslip is missing.**
The run for that month has not been locked yet. Once HR finalizes it, the slip appears here on its own — there is nothing to refresh or request.

**Your year-to-date number differs from what HR quotes you.**
By design: the note on the screen says your view counts "locked (paid) runs only", while HR's console can also include the currently open month. Once the month is finalized, the two agree.

**A deduction you do not recognise.**
Deduction lines are named on the slip — statutory lines are labelled automatically, and any others (for example a loan instalment) were set up by HR. Ask HR to explain a specific line.

**Your net looks lower than usual.**
Check the loss-of-pay line on the slip: it states the days and whether they were "auto-derived from attendance and unpaid or partially-paid leave" or "manually set by HR".

**You cannot edit anything on the slip.**
That is intentional — a locked payslip is the paid, immutable record. Corrections go through HR.

## What happens behind the scenes

- When HR locks a payroll run, each included employee's slip becomes visible here at once, and the year-to-date figures are recomputed from finalized runs only.
- Held nets are not counted as paid until they are released; a released amount pays out as an adjustment line in a later month's slip.
- The print formats only change what the paper looks like — the numbers on screen never change.
