# My account and settings

The Settings screen is your personal control panel — profile, employee contact details, password, notifications, appearance, locale and privacy, all in one place.

## Who can use this

Every signed-in Fernly user. Everything on this screen only ever affects your own account — you will never see anyone else's settings here.

## Where to find it

Sidebar → General → **Settings**.

The screen is a single long page divided into titled sections. Scroll to the one you need. (The onboarding, identification documents, reviews and skill map sections on this page have their own guides — see [Related flows](#related-flows).)

## When to use it

- To correct your name, bio, avatar colour or time zone.
- To update the phone number, location, emergency contact or skills HR keeps on file for you.
- To change your password, or to sign yourself out of every device at once.
- To turn mention notifications on or off.
- To pick an accent colour, dark mode or a compact sidebar.
- To download a copy of the personal data Fernly holds about you.

## Before you start

- Your **Role** field is greyed out on purpose. Hovering it shows the explanation: "Roles are managed by workspace admins". Ask an admin if your role needs changing.
- Your **Email** cannot be edited here — it identifies your account.
- Job data (job title, department, hire date, grade, employment type, branch, employment status) is set by HR and shown read-only in the **Employee profile** section.

## Steps

**Update your profile**
1. In the **Profile** section, edit **Full name**.
2. Pick an **Avatar color** from the swatches next to your avatar.
3. Choose your **Time zone** from the dropdown.
4. Optionally write or edit your **Bio**.
5. Click **Save changes**.

**Update your employee contact details**
1. In the **Employee profile** section, fill in **Phone**, **Location**, **Emergency contact** and **Emergency contact phone**.
2. List your skills in **Skills (comma-separated)** — this quick summary is yours to edit.
3. Click **Save employee profile**.

**Change your password**
1. In the **Security** section, enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter a **New password** of at least 8 characters.
3. Repeat it under **Confirm new password**.
4. Click **Update password**.

**Sign out of every device**
1. In the **Security** section, under **Sign out all devices**, click **Sign out all devices**.
2. When "Are you sure?" appears, click **Yes, sign out everywhere**.

**Change how Fernly looks**
1. In the **Appearance** section, click an **Accent color** (Forest, Ocean, Plum or Ember).
2. Switch **Dark mode** on or off.
3. Switch **Compact sidebar** on for an icon-only rail (same as the keyboard shortcut ⌘B / Ctrl+B).

**Download a copy of your data**
1. Scroll to the **Privacy** section.
2. Click **Download my data**.
3. Wait while the button shows "Preparing export…" — your file downloads automatically.

## What you'll see

The sections appear in this order: **Profile**, **Employee profile**, **My reviews**, **My onboarding**, **My identification documents**, **My skill map**, **Security**, **Notifications**, **Appearance**, **Time & Locale**, **Privacy**.

- **Notifications** lists six rows: **Mentions** ("When someone @mentions you in a task or message") is the only live one — switching it off stops those notifications. **Email notifications**, **Push notifications**, **Due today digest**, **Weekly digest** and **Product updates** each show a **Coming soon** badge and their toggles are switched off — these channels are not connected to a delivery service yet and will start working in a future update.
- **Time & Locale** offers **Time format** (24-hour (14:30) or 12-hour (2:30 PM)) and **First day of week** (Monday or Sunday). These dropdowns do not save to your account yet — the locale setting that is saved is the **Time zone** in the **Profile** section.
- **Privacy** explains that the export covers "your profile, projects, tasks, messages, notifications, reminders, time entries, and audit trail" and arrives as a file downloaded to your device.

## Common problems

**The save button stays greyed out.**
The **Update password** button only enables once the new password is at least 8 characters, matches the confirmation, and differs from your current password. Hints appear under the fields as you type: "Must be at least 8 characters", "Must be different from current", "Passwords don't match".

**You see "Current password is incorrect."**
> Current password is incorrect.

Retype your current password carefully. If you have lost it, use the password reset link on the sign-in screen instead.

**Your new password is refused even though it is long enough.**
> New password must be different from your current password.

Pick something you have not used on this account before.

**You cannot change your own role.**
That is intentional — the **Role** field only displays it. Workspace admins change roles in the Users screen.

**Your job title or department is wrong.**
Those fields sit in the read-only "Job data (set by HR)" strip. Contact HR to correct them.

**The data export fails.**
> Failed to export data

Try again in a moment; if it keeps failing, contact your workspace admin.

## What happens behind the scenes

- Saving your profile also updates your name and avatar colour everywhere your account appears, such as the team roster.
- Changing your password ends your other signed-in sessions and cancels any pending password-reset links, so an old session or link cannot be reused.
- **Sign out all devices** revokes every active session for your account on every browser and device; you are returned to the sign-in screen.
- **Download my data** gathers only the records linked to your own account, builds the file on demand, and sends it straight to your browser — nothing is emailed and nobody else is involved.
