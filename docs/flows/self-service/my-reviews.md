# My reviews and skill map

See where each of your performance review cycles stands, write and submit your self-assessment, acknowledge finished reviews, and view the skill ratings HR maintains for you.

## Who can use this

Every signed-in Fernly user. Both sections show only your own reviews and your own skill ratings.

## Where to find it

Sidebar → General → **Settings**, then scroll to the **My reviews** and **My skill map** sections.

## When to use it

- When a review cycle has been opened for you and you need to write your self-assessment.
- To check the status of a cycle you have already submitted.
- To read your manager's completed review and acknowledge it.
- To see the skill ratings HR has recorded for you.

## Before you start

- Review cycles are opened for you by HR or your manager — you cannot create one yourself.
- A cycle moves through four statuses: **Draft** → **Submitted** → **Reviewed** → **Acknowledged**. You act in Draft (write) and in Reviewed (acknowledge).
- Submitting your self-assessment is final for that cycle — you cannot edit it afterwards, so check it before clicking.
- Your skill map is read-only. The ratings belong to HR; the only skills text you can edit is the free-text **Skills (comma-separated)** field in the **Employee profile** section.

## Steps

**Write and submit a self-assessment**
1. Under **My reviews**, find the cycle whose status is **Draft**.
2. Click **Write**.
3. Type your answers in **Self-assessment** (the prompt suggests: "What went well? What would you do differently? Where do you want to grow?").
4. Optionally choose a **Self rating (1-5)**, or leave it on "No rating".
5. Click **Save & submit**.

**Acknowledge a finished review**
1. Wait until the cycle shows **Reviewed** — the manager's rating and comment are now visible on the row.
2. Click **Acknowledge**.

## What you'll see

Each review row shows the cycle title (with a **Probation** tag for probation reviews), the review period as "start → end", the manager's rating once given ("mgr 4/5"), who reviewed it, and the status badge: **Draft**, **Submitted**, **Reviewed** or **Acknowledged**. When a cycle is Reviewed, the manager's comment is quoted on the row. If no cycles exist yet you see "No review cycles have been opened for you yet."

The self-assessment window repeats the period and warns: "submitting locks your answers and notifies your manager."

**My skill map** lists each rated skill with its rating chip — for example "4/5 · Advanced" — plus who assessed it ("assessed by …" or "not yet assessed"), the assessment date and any note. The rating ladder is: 1 Learning, 2 Developing, 3 Solid, 4 Advanced, 5 Expert. A summary line above the list reads like "3 skills · avg 3.7 (Solid)". If nothing is rated yet: "No rated skills yet — the free-text summary stays on the profile."

## Common problems

**You cannot write your self-assessment.**
Only cycles in **Draft** show a **Write** button. Once submitted, editing is refused:
> This cycle is already Submitted — the self-assessment can no longer be edited.

(The message names the cycle's current status.)

**You try to submit twice.**
> Only a draft cycle can be submitted — this one is submitted.

**You cannot acknowledge a review.**
Acknowledging only appears on a **Reviewed** cycle, via the **Acknowledge** button:
> Only a reviewed cycle can be acknowledged — this one is acknowledged.

**You cannot change a rating in your skill map.**
That is by design — the section header says "Your rated skills — HR maintains the ratings; edit your free-text summary above". Ask HR if a rating looks wrong. Your own editable summary lives in **Employee profile** → **Skills (comma-separated)**.

**Your self-assessment is gone.**
It is not — after submission the cycle simply locks. Your manager sees your text when completing the review.

## What happens behind the scenes

- Submitting your self-assessment locks your answers and notifies your manager chain that it is ready for review.
- Your manager (or another reviewer in your chain) then completes the review with a comment and a 1–5 rating, which is what you see quoted on the **Reviewed** row.
- Acknowledging records that you have read the finished review; the cycle then shows **Acknowledged** and is closed.
- Skill ratings are maintained by HR in the employee directory; each rating records who assessed it and when.
