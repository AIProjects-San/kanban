# Signing your offer online

This guide is for job candidates. When a company offers you a job through Fernly, you get one email with one private link — that link is the offer, and this page explains how to read it, sign it, or turn it down. You do not need an account, a password, or any app: everything happens in your web browser.

## Who can use this

You, if you have received an offer email from a hiring team that uses Fernly. Nobody else — the link works only for the person it was sent to.

## Where to find it

In your email inbox. Look for a message from the company's hiring team with a subject like "Your offer from <company> — <job title>". The email contains a long web address (a link) ending in `/offer/` followed by a long code. Click it, or copy the whole address into your browser. That link is the only way in — there is nothing to sign into.

## When to use it

- You received an offer email and want to read the offer.
- You have decided to accept and are ready to sign.
- You have decided to decline.

## Before you start

- The link is personal. Anyone who has it can sign or decline the offer in your name, so do not forward the email or share the link with anyone.
- The link expires 7 days after it was sent. After that, ask the hiring team for a fresh one.
- The link works once. After you sign or decline, it shows your recorded answer and cannot be used again.
- You do not need to create any account to sign.

## Steps

1. Open the offer email and click the link inside it.
2. Read the letter at the top — it is the formal offer text the hiring team wrote.
3. Review "Your annual package" — each pay line with its yearly amount, then "Net take-home / yr" and "Total package (CTC) / yr" — and the "Benefits" list if there is one.
4. To accept: in the "Sign this offer" box, type your full legal name exactly as it appears on the offer.
5. Click **Sign offer**.
6. To decline instead: click **Decline**.
7. After signing, click **Print appointment letter** if you want a copy for your records.

## What you'll see

- The page headline: "<your name>, your offer awaits your signature", with "Review everything below, then sign at the bottom. Nothing changes until you do."
- The letter, the package numbers, and the benefits.
- The "Sign this offer" box with one text field ("Your full legal name"), the **Sign offer** button, the **Decline** button, and a note: "Link expires <date> · single use".
- After signing, a green confirmation — "You're signed — welcome aboard!" — saying your acceptance was recorded with a timestamp and cannot be changed, and the company will be in touch about your start date and first day. Below it is your "Letter of Appointment": the letter again, the package, and your recorded signature ("Accepted electronically" — your name, date and time, "For and on behalf of <company>").
- After declining, a plain screen headed "Offer declined" saying the decision was recorded, and to reply to the offer email or contact the hiring team if it was a mistake.
- If you re-open the link later, you see your recorded outcome — the confirmation if you signed, the declined screen if you declined.

## Common problems

**The name will not accept.** The typed name must match the name on the offer (capitalisation does not matter):

> Please type your full legal name exactly as it appears on the offer.

**The link no longer opens.** If the address was mistyped, cut short, or does not match an offer any more, you see:

> This offer link is not valid.

**The link ran out of time.** Links last 7 days:

> This offer link has expired — please ask HR to resend it.

The page also tells you: "Signing links are single-use and expire after 7 days. If you were expecting an offer, reach out to the hiring team — they can resend a fresh link."

**Someone forwarded you their link.** Do not use it — signing someone else's offer records their name. The page footer warns: "This link is personal — forwarded copies cannot be signed twice."

**You want to change something in the offer.** Nothing changes until you sign — close the page and reply to the offer email instead. Once signed, the acceptance is recorded and cannot be changed online; raise any correction with the hiring team.

**The page says the offer is not available yet.**

> This offer is not available for signing yet.

This means the link reached you before the offer was fully sent. Wait a moment and open it again, or ask the hiring team.

## What happens behind the scenes

When you click **Sign offer**, your typed name, the date and time, and your connection details are recorded as your electronic signature, and the offer is locked — it can never be edited or signed again. Declining is recorded the same way. The hiring team sees the signed or declined state immediately on their side and will contact you about next steps. You do not need to do anything else in Fernly to start the job — the company handles your setup.
