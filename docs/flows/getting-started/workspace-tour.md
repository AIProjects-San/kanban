# Workspace tour

A quick orientation: the sidebar, the top bar, keyboard shortcuts and the Help screen. Once you know these four things, every other guide in this documentation will make sense.

## Who can use this

Every signed-in user.

## Where to find it

Sign in (see [Signing in](sign-in.md)) — you land directly in the workspace.

## When to use it

- On your first day, before following any other guide.
- Whenever you cannot find a screen you have read about.

## Before you start

Fernly's workspace is a single screen with a **left sidebar**. Clicking a sidebar item switches the main area to that view. One important detail: **the workspace switches views without changing the web address**. When a guide in this documentation says "Sidebar → People → Leave", it means: click **Leave** under the **People** group in the left sidebar. You cannot bookmark or share a link to a specific view — only to the workspace itself.

## Steps

1. Look at the left sidebar. It is organised into groups — **Overview**, **Work**, **People**, **HR** (roles with HR access only), **Administration** (admins only) and **General**. Click **Tasks** under **Work** to switch views; click **Dashboard** to come back.
2. Click the sidebar toggle button at the far left of the top bar — the sidebar collapses to a narrow icon rail. Click it again to expand it back with labels.
3. Press `⌘`/`Ctrl` + `K` (or click **Search or jump to…** in the top bar) to open the command palette. Type a view, project or action name, then press `Enter`. Press `Esc` to close it.
4. Click the **Inbox** button (top bar) to open your direct messages; click the **bell** to see recent notifications such as assignments, mentions and approvals.
5. Click your name (top right) to open the account menu — your profile details and the **Sign out** button live there.
6. Open Sidebar → General → **Help**. Browse the how-to articles and click the keyboard-shortcuts window (or just press `?` anywhere) to see every shortcut Fernly offers.
7. When you are done, click your account menu and choose **Sign out**. Your work is saved as you go.

## What you'll see

**The sidebar groups and their items:**

| Group | Items |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Dashboard, Analytics |
| **Work** | Projects, Tasks, Bugs |
| **People** | Team, Calendar, Leave, My Interviews, My Payslips, My Attendance |
| **HR** | HR Console, Hiring, Payroll *(only for roles with HR access)* |
| **Administration** | Users, Workspace Config, Audit Log *(admins only)* |
| **General** | Settings, Help |

Items you lack the role for are simply not shown — see [Roles](roles.md).

**The top bar**, from left to right: the sidebar toggle, **Search or jump to…** (command palette), **Inbox** (direct messages, with unread count), the **bell** (notifications), the light/dark theme toggle, and your account menu with name and role.

**The keyboard shortcuts** (full list under Help → Keyboard shortcuts, or press `?`):

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| `⌘`/`Ctrl` + `K` | Open the command palette |
| `⌘`/`Ctrl` + `B` | Toggle the sidebar |
| `?` | Show keyboard shortcuts |
| `N` | New task |
| `P` | New project |
| `B` | Open notifications |
| `I` | Open inbox |
| `G` then `D` | Go to Dashboard |
| `G` then `T` | Go to Tasks |
| `G` then `V` | Go to Leave |
| `G` then `N` | Go to My Payslips |
| `G` then `S` | Go to Settings |
| `Esc` | Close the open modal or palette |

"Go to" shortcuts exist for every sidebar item — press `G`, release, then press the item's letter within a second.

**The Help screen** (Sidebar → General → **Help**) contains short how-to articles grouped by category (Getting Started, Tasks & Projects, Team & Collaboration, Account & Settings), a **Keyboard shortcuts** window, and support contacts (email support and community links).

## Common problems

**The view you expected is not in the sidebar.**
Either your role does not include it (see [Roles](roles.md)) or the sidebar is collapsed to the icon rail — click the sidebar toggle to expand it and show labels.

**You followed "Sidebar → X" but see something else.**
Views do not change the web address, so a browser "back" does not undo a view switch the way you might expect. Just click the intended sidebar item again.

**You cannot find a specific project, task or person.**
Use the command palette (`⌘`/`Ctrl` + `K`) and type its name.

**You want to leave Fernly for the day.**
Click your account menu (top right) and choose **Sign out**. Your work is saved as you go.

## What happens behind the scenes

Nothing you do in this tour changes data. The workspace keeps your current view, sidebar state and appearance preference on your device, so Fernly reopens the way you left it. Signing out ends the session on that device only.
