# Signing in to Fernly

Sign in to reach your workspace — your tasks, leave, payslips and everything else Fernly manages for your team.

## Who can use this

Anyone with a Fernly account. Your admin creates your account — there is no sign-up page. If you were hired through Fernly's hiring pipeline, your account is created automatically when HR marks you as joined.

## Where to find it

Open your workspace address in any modern browser (for example `https://app.fernly.app`, or the address your company gave you). If you are already signed in, you land straight in the workspace — see [Workspace tour](workspace-tour.md) to sign out.

## When to use it

- At the start of your work day, or whenever your session has ended.
- After an admin has created or re-enabled your account.
- After your first day, once your account exists (new hires: see below).

## Before you start

- You need an **account**. Your admin creates it and gives you your email address and starting password. If you are unsure whether you have an account, ask your admin — there is no sign-up page and no "forgot password" link.
- Use the same browser window; Fernly keeps you signed in on that device until you sign out.

## Steps

1. Open your workspace address.
2. Enter the **Email** address of your account.
3. Enter your **Password**.
4. Click **Sign in**.

## What you'll see

- While signed out, the sign-in screen shows the heading **Welcome back** with the line "Sign in to your workspace to continue.", plus the **Email** and **Password** fields and the **Sign in** button.
- After a successful sign-in, you land on the workspace with the left sidebar (see [Workspace tour](workspace-tour.md)).
- If the credentials are wrong, a pop-up message appears at the top of the screen:

   > Invalid email or password.

- While Fernly is checking your details, the **Sign in** button shows a spinner — wait a moment rather than clicking again.

## Common problems

**"Invalid email or password." keeps appearing.**
Double-check for typos, and that you are using the email address your admin registered for you (a work email, or for new hires the address Fernly generated — see [Marking a candidate as joined](../recruitment/mark-joined.md)). If the password was recently changed, use the newest one your admin gave you.

**You cannot sign in even though the password looks right.**
After **5 failed attempts within 15 minutes**, Fernly temporarily refuses further sign-in attempts for that email address (and from that network). Waiting 15 minutes clears it. This is a safety measure against password guessing, not a problem with your account.

**You forgot your password.**
Password reset is not available — contact your admin. An admin can set a new password for you from the Users screen (see [Managing users](../admin/users.md)).

**You do not have an account yet.**
Ask your admin to create one. Fernly has no public sign-up.

## What happens behind the scenes

Fernly checks your email and password against your workspace's account list. Repeated failures are counted per email address and per network, and temporarily block further attempts to slow down password guessing. A record of the sign-in is kept in the workspace's audit trail. When you sign in successfully, Fernly remembers your session on that device until you sign out.
