# Dashboard and analytics

A read-only overview of your workspace: KPI cards, activity and payslip shortcuts on the Dashboard, plus performance charts on the Analytics view.

## Who can use this

Every signed-in user. What you see is scoped to your role: Admins see the whole workspace; Managers and Members see only the projects they own, are staffed on, or have tasks in. HR users see their own numbers.

## Where to find it

- Dashboard: Sidebar → Overview → **Dashboard**
- Analytics: Sidebar → Overview → **Analytics** (or click **View details** on the Dashboard's Project Analytics card)

## When to use it

- At the start of your day, to see your open tasks, reminders due today and recent activity.
- In a check-in or review, to look at throughput, hours tracked and top contributors.
- To jump quickly to a running project, your payslips, or the team list.

## Before you start

Both views are read-only — nothing you click here changes data. Reminders on the Dashboard are display-only; there is no screen that creates or edits them. The Dashboard greeting ("Good morning", "Good afternoon" or "Good evening") follows your timezone setting.

## Steps

**Reading the Dashboard**

1. Open Sidebar → Overview → **Dashboard**.
2. Read the four KPI cards: **Total Projects**, **Ended**, **Running** and **Pending**.
3. Check the Project Analytics card — the **Weekly Completion** ring and the **Project Ended**, **In Progress** and **Pending** progress bars.
4. Click a project under **Current Projects** to open its detail window.
5. Glance at the right-hand cards: **Latest payslip** (if you have one), **Reminders**, **Team Collaboration** and **Recent Activity**.
6. If your role allows it, use **Add Project** or **New Task** in the header to jump straight into creating one — see [Projects](projects.md) and [Tasks](tasks.md).

**Reading Analytics**

1. Open Sidebar → Overview → **Analytics**.
2. If you manage people or are an Admin, pick who the numbers cover from the filter next to the heading: **Just me**, **My team**, **Everyone** (Admins only), or a specific person under **People**.
3. Choose a period with the **7D**, **30D** or **90D** buttons.
4. Read the four metric cards: **Tasks completed**, **Hours tracked**, **On-time delivery** and **Avg. cycle time**.
5. Scroll through the **Throughput** chart, **Time by project** bars, **Top contributors** list and the **Activity** heatmap.
6. Click **Export CSV** to download the throughput figures as a spreadsheet.

## What you'll see

- On the Dashboard, the subtitle under the greeting reads "You have N open tasks and M reminders today" — your own tasks, not the workspace's.
- The **Latest payslip** card appears only once you have a finalized payslip, and links to your payslip history via **My Payslips**.
- Overdue reminders are flagged with an "Overdue" marker; the Reminders card shows "All clear!" when nothing is due.
- On Analytics, each metric card shows a small change indicator (for example "+8%"). **Top contributors** hides itself when the filter covers a single person. The **Activity** heatmap shows hours per day over the last 13 weeks with a **Less**/**More** colour legend.
- Empty periods show "No time tracked in this period" and "No members in this scope yet."

## Common problems

**The Analytics screen shows an error instead of charts.**

Your role limits whose data you may view. The messages you can see are:

> Only admins can view workspace-wide analytics.

> You can only view your own team's analytics.

> You can only view analytics for yourself and your team.

Switch the filter back to **Just me** (or **My team** if you are a Manager) and reload the view.

**No projects appear on the Dashboard.**

Either your role cannot create projects (the card invites you to "Ask an admin or manager to create one.") or you are not yet staffed on any project. See [Projects](projects.md).

**Numbers look lower than expected.**

Analytics only counts the last 7, 30 or 90 days depending on the period buttons, and only for the person or team selected in the filter. The Dashboard's "Current Projects" list is capped at five running projects.

## What happens behind the scenes

No data is saved from these views. **Export CSV** writes a file to your computer containing the dates, tasks completed and hours tracked shown in the Throughput chart. Hours figures come from time logged on tasks (see [Tasks](tasks.md)); the Tasks completed figure is estimated from those logged hours, and the On-time delivery and Avg. cycle time cards show fixed reference values in this release. Every change you see under Recent Activity was generated by an earlier action — for example a task assignment, an approval or a mention — not by the Dashboard itself.
