# Calendar

See everything scheduled in one month grid — meetings, deadlines, reviews, social events, focus blocks and approved leave — and add your own events.

## Who can use this

Everyone can view the calendar. Managers and Admins can create and delete events; Members and HR roles have a read-only calendar.

## Where to find it

Sidebar → People → **Calendar** (or press `G` then `C`).

## When to use it

- Checking what is coming up this week or month.
- Scheduling a meeting, deadline, review, social event or focus block.
- Seeing at a glance who is on approved leave.

## Before you start

The calendar is shared across the workspace — an event you create is visible to everyone. Event types are **Meeting**, **Deadline**, **Review**, **Social**, **Focus** and **Leave**. The **Leave** type also appears automatically for approved leave requests; those entries come from the leave system and cannot be edited or deleted here (see [Leave](../self-service/leave.md)). There is no edit function for events — to change one, delete it and create it again.

## Steps

**Browsing the month**

1. Open Sidebar → People → **Calendar**.
2. Move between months with the arrow buttons (**Previous month** / **Next month**).
3. Click **Today** to jump back to the current month and select today.
4. Click any day cell to select it — its events appear in the panel on the right.
5. Check **Coming up** under the day panel for the next five events, and the colour legend under the grid for event types.

**Creating an event**

1. Click **New Event** (or **+ Add event** in an empty day panel).
2. Enter a **Title** (required).
3. Set the **Date** and **Time**.
4. Choose a **Duration (min)**: 30, 45, 60, 90 or 120.
5. Choose a **Type** from the list above.
6. Optionally enter a **Location** (for example "Google Meet, Room A, etc.").
7. Click **Create event**.

**Reviewing or deleting an event**

1. Click an event in the day panel (or a chip on the grid) to open its details.
2. Review the time, duration, type, location and date.
3. Expand **History & Audit** to see when it was created or changed.
4. Click **Delete event** to remove it, or **Close** to leave it.
5. From the day panel you can also hover an event row and click **Delete** directly.

**Changing an event**

1. Open the event and note its details.
2. Click **Delete event**.
3. Create a new event with the corrected details, as above.

## What you'll see

- The header summarises: "N events scheduled · Click a day to see details".
- A Monday-first month grid; today's date is circled, the selected day is outlined, and days from adjacent months are shown muted. Each cell shows up to three event chips, then "+N more".
- The right-hand panel is headed with the selected weekday and date, listing that day's events with their time, duration and location; when empty it shows "No events scheduled" with an **+ Add event** link.
- **Coming up** shows the next five events with a date block, time and type; when empty, "Nothing on the horizon."
- Approved leave appears as violet **Leave** chips such as "Ada Lovelace — Annual Leave".

## Common problems

**You cannot save an event.**

> Title is required

Give the event a name. If nothing happens at all, your role may not allow creating events — the **New Event** button performs a check on save; ask a Manager or Admin to add the event.

**An event needs its time or duration changed.**

There is no edit function. Delete the event and create a new one with the right details — the change is recorded in the event's audit history.

**You cannot delete a leave entry.**

Leave entries come from approved leave requests, not the calendar. If the leave is cancelled or rejected, the entry disappears on its own; otherwise it stays. See [Leave](../self-service/leave.md).

**An event you expected is missing.**

The grid and **Coming up** list focus on current and upcoming dates — past events remain on their day cells when you navigate back to that month. Also check you are in the right month; clicking **Today** resets the view.

## What happens behind the scenes

Creating an event saves it to the shared workspace calendar and records an audit entry, which feeds the **History & Audit** section in its detail window. Deleting removes it permanently and logs the deletion. Approved leave is not stored on the calendar at all — it is added to the calendar view automatically from the leave system whenever a request is approved, so it always matches the current leave state. No notifications or invitations are sent for calendar events; colleagues simply see them on the grid.
