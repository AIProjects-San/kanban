# Fernly User Guide

How to get things done in Fernly — requesting leave, running payroll, hiring, timesheets, filings and everything in between. Written for the people who use Fernly at work: employees, managers, HR and payroll administrators. No technical knowledge is assumed.

## How to use this guide

Each guide follows the same structure: who can use it, where to find it, when to use it, what to do first, the steps, what you'll see, common problems (with the exact messages Fernly shows), and what happens behind the scenes.

One thing to know before you start: **Fernly's workspace switches views client-side without changing the URL. When this guide says "Sidebar → X", it means click the item in the left sidebar.** The main workspace is one screen; the sidebar groups (Overview, Work, People, HR, Administration, General) switch what it shows. Some administrative areas are separate pages with their own addresses — those guides give the address and where its link lives.

## Glossary

- **Advance (salary advance)** — a short-term payment recovered from the next one to twenty-four pay runs; unlike a loan it carries no interest or schedule.
- **Arrears** — pay owed for a past period, detected when a salary change or correction applies to an already-paid month and booked into a future run.
- **Attendance record** — HR's mark for one person on one day (present, work-from-home, on leave, absent…). Employees cannot self-mark; corrections go to HR.
- **Comp-off (compensatory leave)** — a day off claimed for overtime worked, validated against rostered days.
- **Deduction** — anything subtracted from gross pay: PAYE, KiwiSaver, student loan, loan repayments, loss-of-pay.
- **Designation** — a job title in the employee masters, optionally linked to a grade.
- **ED filing (Employee Details)** — the monthly notification to IRD of employees joining or leaving.
- **EI filing (Employment Information)** — the per-pay-run file uploaded to IRD summarising what everyone was paid.
- **Encashment** — converting unused leave days into a payment, subject to each leave type's rules.
- **Employee profile** — the HR record behind a team member: employee number, department, designation, grade, leave approver.
- **F&F (full-and-final) statement** — the settlement prepared when someone leaves: final pay, dues, deductions.
- **Four-eyes rule** — a change that needs a second person: you cannot approve your own timesheet, transfer, promotion, offer or loan.
- **Grade** — a rung on the salary ladder; may carry a band (minimum–maximum annual pay).
- **Gratuity** — a long-service payout calculated from a rule's slab table.
- **IRD** — Inland Revenue, New Zealand's tax authority; also an employee's IRD number (8–9 digits with a check digit).
- **Joining gate** — an optional workspace rule that blocks marking a candidate joined until their mandatory documents are verified and background checks are finished.
- **Leave period** — the window leave is counted in (allocation, accrual, expiry).
- **LOP / LWP (loss of pay)** — unpaid absence, pro-rated into the payslip.
- **Locked run** — a payroll run marked paid; its payslips are immutable and consumable by filings and loan settlement.
- **myIR** — IRD's online portal, where EI files are uploaded.
- **Open run** — a payroll run still being edited; payslips can change.
- **PAYE** — pay-as-you-earn income tax withheld from salaries.
- **Payslip** — one employee's pay for one run: earnings, deductions, net.
- **Retention bonus** — a staged payment tied to staying for an agreed period.
- **Roster** — the schedule of who is on which shift, edited per day.
- **Salary structure** — an employee's annual salary components; the monthly gross is the total divided by twelve.
- **Statutory rules** — the versioned rate tables (PAYE, KiwiSaver, student loan, ACC) a tax year points at.
- **Tax code** — the IR330 declaration (M, M SL, STC…) that selects the PAYE table applied to a payslip.
- **Timesheet** — an employee's actual hours for a period, drafted by the employee, approved by HR (four-eyes), then consumable by payroll.
- **Trial balance** — the accounting report of every ledger account's balances for a period; corrections after closing are reversal entries.
- **Withholding** — pay deliberately held back from a run and released later.

## Known limitations

Honest gaps in the current release, so you are not surprised by them:

1. **No password self-reset (P2).** The sign-in screen has no "Forgot password?" — and the welcome email sent to new hires points at one that does not exist. An admin must set every initial or forgotten password from [Users](admin/users.md). Tracked as a P2 UX gap.
2. **Salary history is Super Admin only (P2).** Payroll admins cannot currently access the [salary history](payroll/salary-history.md) page even though it is payroll data — P2 backlog.
3. **No deep links to workspace views (P2).** The main workspace switches views without changing the address, so you cannot bookmark or share a link to a specific view (Projects, Tasks, HR Console…). Only the separate admin pages have addresses.
4. **Employees cannot view their own loans (P2).** Loan repayments appear only as deduction lines on [payslips](self-service/my-payslips.md); there is no self-service loan view.
5. **Biometric adapter not configured (P3, intentional).** The biometric screens are ready (registry, mappings, punch log, exceptions) but live device sync is not switched on. Use [CSV import](attendance/biometric-punches.md) until hardware is connected.
6. **Demo IRD numbers must be replaced before real filing (pre-launch).** Seeded demo data carries IRD numbers outside IRD's issued range — they pass validation but trigger myIR warnings. Replace every employee's [tax profile](statutory/employee-tax-profiles.md) IRD number before your first real filing.
7. **Employer IRD must be configured before real filing (pre-launch).** The [filing profile](statutory/filings.md) needs your real employer IRD number and payroll contact before generating IRD files.
